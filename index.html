      Good to know

      Decoding happens in your browser using your device's processor. No photo or camera frame is sent to a server.

      Camera access needs an HTTPS address. On a local file or plain HTTP the browser will block it, and only image upload will work.

      Open a scanned link only if you recognise where it goes. QR codes in public places are sometimes swapped by scammers.
